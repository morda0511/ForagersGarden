# Changelog

## 1.1.0

### NEW FEATURES
- **Decoration trees** in the Cultivator, under the new **Decor** button of the build menu: 16 tree kinds (beech, birch, autumn birch, oak, fir, big fir, pine, snow pines and firs, Ashlands trees) in three sizes each: small, half-grown and grown. They are decoration only: they do not grow, cannot be chopped and give no wood. The hammer removes them and returns the materials. They cost the seeds or cones of the kind (2 / 4 / 6), the Ashlands trees cost wood. Each one has its own picture as its icon
- New setting **DecorTrees** (on by default) to switch them off

## 1.0.2

### CHANGES
- Planted herbs (Dandelion, Thistle, Fiddlehead, Smoke puff) are gone after you pick them, like a field crop. Before, they grew back on the same spot like the wild ones and could not be removed. Herbs already planted follow this too: the ones waiting to regrow are removed when the world loads
- New setting **HerbsRegrow** (off by default): on = herbs grow back like before
- Berry bushes and mushrooms are unchanged

## 1.0.1

### NEW FEATURES
- The Lingonberry bush (new in Valheim 1.0) can be planted with the Cultivator, like the other berry bushes

## 1.0.0

### NEW FEATURES
- First version: the berry bushes, wild herbs, mushrooms and the Ice, Royal Jelly and Crystal beehives from StoreAndCraft as their own mod. Everything built with StoreAndCraft 1.5 or older keeps working
- Switches for the beehives and for the Cultivator plants: off = not offered any more, built ones keep working
