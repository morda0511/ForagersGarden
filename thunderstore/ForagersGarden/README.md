**What you forage, you can grow.**

Plant berry bushes, wild herbs and mushrooms with the Cultivator, decorate with trees in three sizes, and keep bees that make ice, royal jelly and crystal.

> **Install it on every client and on the server.** Valheim 1.0 · BepInExPack 5.4.2350+ · Console players cannot load the mod.

### Plants
- **Berry bushes:** Raspberry, Blueberry, Cloudberry, Lingonberry (a sapling costs 5 berries)
- **Wild herbs:** Dandelion, Thistle, Fiddlehead, Smoke puff (a sapling costs 1, gives 3 per harvest)
- **Mushrooms:** Mushroom, Yellow mushroom, Blue mushroom. Plant a mushroom to grow **spores**, plant the spores to grow mushrooms
- They stand there grown right away. A planted herb is gone after you pick it, like a field crop (plant it again, or let the scarecrow do it). The bushes stay and grow their berries back
- Only on cultivated ground

### Decoration trees
- Open the Cultivator and pick the **Decor** button: every tree kind as a **small**, a **half-grown** and a **grown** tree, each with its own picture as icon
- Beech, birch, autumn birch, oak, fir, big fir, pine, snow pines and firs, and the Ashlands trees
- Decoration only: they do not grow, cannot be chopped and give no wood. Remove them with the **hammer** and you get the materials back
- They cost the seeds or cones of the kind (2 / 4 / 6), the Ashlands trees cost wood

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/morda0511/ForagersGarden/main/thunderstore-assets/foragers-garden/dividers/divider-beehives-hammer-green.png)

### Beehives
- Craft an **Ice**, **Royal Jelly** or **Crystal Queen Bee** at the workbench (Queen Bee + Ice / Royal Jelly / Crystal)
- Build the white / yellow / violet beehive with it in the hammer. It makes ice / royal jelly / crystal at the pace of honey, the bees take its colour

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/morda0511/ForagersGarden/main/thunderstore-assets/foragers-garden/dividers/divider-settings-green.png)

The file is `BepInEx/config/com.morda.foragersgarden.cfg`, or use the Morda Mod Manager (**F10**).

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Beehives | on | Ice, Royal Jelly and Crystal beehives and their queens. Off = not offered any more, hives you built keep working |
| CultivatorPlants | on | Berry bushes, wild herbs, mushrooms and spores in the Cultivator. Off = not offered any more, plants you planted keep growing |
| DecorTrees | on | The decoration trees in the Cultivator. Off = not offered any more, trees you built stay |
| HerbsRegrow | off | On = planted herbs grow their harvest back like the wild ones instead of disappearing when picked |

The vanilla beehive and vanilla crops are never changed.

### With Store & Craft (optional)
- Forager's Garden **runs on its own**, Store & Craft is not needed
- With **Store & Craft 1.6.0** or newer both work together: the scarecrow plants and harvests the bushes, herbs and mushrooms, auto-store (**N**) works on the beehives like on honey
- Older Store & Craft versions (1.5 and older) had this content built in: Forager's Garden stays off next to them until Store & Craft is updated

### Coming from Store & Craft 1.5 or older
- Update Store & Craft to 1.6.0 and install Forager's Garden at the same time. The names are the same, so everything you built stays where it is, queens and spores in your chests too
