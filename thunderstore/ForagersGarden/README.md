# Forager's Garden

**What you forage, you can grow.**
Plant berry bushes, wild herbs and mushrooms with the Cultivator, and keep bees that make ice, royal jelly and crystal.

**Install it on every client and on the server.** Valheim 1.0 · BepInExPack 5.4.2350+.

---

## Plants (Cultivator)
- **Berry bushes:** Raspberry, Blueberry, Cloudberry, Lingonberry (a sapling costs 5 berries)
- **Wild herbs:** Dandelion, Thistle, Fiddlehead, Smoke puff (a sapling costs 1, gives 3 per harvest)
- They stand there grown right away and grow their harvest back
- **Mushrooms:** Mushroom, Yellow mushroom, Blue mushroom. Plant a mushroom to grow **spores**, plant the spores to grow mushrooms
- Only on cultivated ground

## Beehives (hammer)
- Craft an **Ice**, **Royal Jelly** or **Crystal Queen Bee** at the workbench (Queen Bee + Ice / Royal Jelly / Crystal)
- Build the white / yellow / violet beehive with it. It makes ice / royal jelly / crystal at the pace of honey, the bees take its colour

## Settings
- **Beehives** and **CultivatorPlants** (config file, or the Morda Mod Manager with F10): off = they are not offered any more. Hives and plants you already built keep working
- The vanilla beehive and vanilla crops are never changed

## With StoreAndCraft (optional)
- Forager's Garden **runs on its own**, StoreAndCraft is not needed
- With **StoreAndCraft 1.6.0** or newer both work together: the scarecrow plants and harvests the bushes, herbs and mushrooms, auto-store (**N**) works on the beehives like on honey
- Older StoreAndCraft versions (1.5 and older) had this content built in: Forager's Garden stays off next to them until StoreAndCraft is updated

## Coming from StoreAndCraft 1.5 or older
- Update StoreAndCraft to 1.6.0 and install Forager's Garden at the same time. The names are the same, so everything you built stays where it is, queens and spores in your chests too

## Install
1. Install [BepInExPack Valheim](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/denikson/BepInExPack_Valheim/)
2. Put `ForagersGarden.dll` in `BepInEx/plugins/ForagersGarden/` (or use a mod manager)
